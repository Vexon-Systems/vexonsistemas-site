# Fundos ilustrativos das heros

Imagens geradas com a ferramenta integrada `image_gen`, como conceitos temporários. Não representam clientes, a equipe real ou uma instalação de telemetria já realizada.

Cada cena tem um WebP horizontal (1536 × 1024) e um recorte vertical (512 × 768). O navegador carrega somente a versão adequada por meio de picture/source. `planning-mobile.webp` é um recorte da imagem ilustrativa de planejamento já existente, cuja origem está em `../home/README.md`.

- `commerce-desktop.webp` / `commerce-mobile.webp`: loja virtual e contexto comercial do SellionPDV.
- `software-desktop.webp` / `software-mobile.webp`: desenvolvimento, projetos e organização digital do ConsultorHub.
- `irrigation-desktop.webp` / `irrigation-mobile.webp`: contexto de pesquisa da Telemetria de Pivôs.

## Prompt — loja virtual

Use case: photorealistic-natural. Wide 3:2 editorial photograph for the background of a Brazilian online-store development website. Real small business e-commerce workspace with unbranded kraft shipping boxes, a simple blank product package and graphite laptop on a dark charcoal table. Laptop screen has a quiet minimal online shop layout with product photographs, NO readable text, logos, prices, numbers or charts. Main laptop and packages in center-right with generous surroundings, dark empty left third for white headings. Warm natural side light, restrained neutral colors, one muted burgundy notebook. Frame so central narrow portrait crop is an attractive close view of a package and laptop keyboard, without needing screen text or a face. Authentic tactile cardboard, realistic hardware. No people, no neon, no holograms, no decorative floating UI, no watermarks, no text burned into photo. This is an illustrative scene, not a client case.

## Prompt — software

Use case: photorealistic-natural. Wide 3:2 sophisticated editorial photograph for a Brazilian custom software development studio website hero background. Dark modest real developer workspace, graphite laptop slightly right of center, laptop screen showing softly defocused lines of source code in a dark editor, no readable words, no dashboards, no charts, no logos. One natural hand resting on keyboard entering code, no face, no identifiable person. Small closed neutral notebook. Clean almost empty charcoal background on the left third reserved for white headline. Soft window side lighting, warm natural skin tone, real matte metal keyboard and desk texture, calm understated palette. Moderate depth of field; central portrait crop should still show a meaningful close-up of coding hands and keyboard. No neon, no glowing RGB gaming equipment, no cyberpunk, no floating graphics, no watermarks, no burned-in headline. Temporary illustrative photograph, not the actual team.

## Prompt — irrigação

Use case: photorealistic-natural. Wide 3:2 editorial agricultural photograph as website hero background for a research project on center-pivot irrigation telemetry in Minas Gerais Brazil. A real metal center pivot irrigation structure extending diagonally through a green crop field, modest rural Brazilian landscape, warm early morning light, soft light mist from irrigation water. No identifiable farm or person. Lower horizon, calm darker green foreground and dark open space in the left third for white headings, machinery is center-right. Keep a recognizable tower in central crop so both wide landscape and narrow portrait crops work beautifully. Authentic farming photography, realistic engineering detail, muted natural greens and warm sky, professional and restrained. No drones shown, no charts, no digital overlays, no sensors or validated software capabilities implied, no branding, no text, no watermark. Illustrative research context, not a real deployed client project.
