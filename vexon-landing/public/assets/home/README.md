## Fundo responsivo da hero

A hero usa `hero-workspace-desktop.webp` (1536 × 1024, cerca de 56 KB) e `hero-workspace-mobile.webp` (768 × 1152, cerca de 30 KB). O navegador seleciona apenas a versão adequada via picture/source, com prioridade alta. Ambas são imagens ilustrativas geradas pela ferramenta integrada image_gen. A versão vertical foi editada a partir da horizontal, para reservar espaço atrás do texto e preservar o notebook na parte inferior. O antigo site-devices-1200.webp foi mantido como arquivo anterior, mas não é mais usado.

### Prompt da versão horizontal

Use case: photorealistic-natural. Create a sophisticated photographic background for the hero of Vexon, a small Brazilian website and software studio. Landscape composition 3:2, designed to crop gracefully to a narrow portrait phone viewport. A calm real workspace: graphite laptop on a dark matte desk, photographed from a low oblique angle; laptop placed slightly right of center, screen showing a quiet minimal website design with a warm architectural photograph and off-white blocks, NO readable letters, NO brand names, NO statistics or charts. A simple paper notebook and pencil next to it. Laptop is secondary atmosphere rather than an enormous product close-up; whole laptop stays in middle half of image with generous space around it. Left third is very dark softly defocused room with NO objects, reserved for white website headlines. Center and right contain subtle tactile details and a small pool of warm natural window light on desk. Background charcoal, graphite and warm neutral tones with subtle muted burgundy notebook accent. Authentic editorial photography, restrained, softly lit, real materials, moderate depth of field. Must remain attractive when only the middle 40 percent of width is visible on mobile: central crop should show laptop and desk without relying on readable screen content. No phone, no floating UI, no glow, no neon, no particles, no gradients, no logos, no prominent text, no busy office props, no people, no watermarks. No website title or caption burned into the image.

### Prompt da versão vertical

Edit target: the supplied workspace photograph. Create a portrait 2:3 version for a mobile website hero background. Preserve the same graphite laptop, neutral minimal webpage with no text, dark desk, warm light, muted burgundy notebook, photographic realism and color palette. Recompose for mobile: upper 55 percent of the portrait image must be an almost-empty very dark charcoal softly defocused wall, reserved for white headline and paragraph. Place the laptop and notebook in the lower 40 percent of the portrait frame, with the ENTIRE laptop visible and smaller than in the reference, not an enlarged crop. No lettering, no logos, no captions, no numbers, no new props. Leave clear dark space behind the primary website button above the laptop. Quiet premium editorial photography, believable perspective. The image itself has no overlaid UI or headline. Portrait canvas 2:3.
